# 12-Month Content Plan

Prioritized by a mix of search intent and business value — transactional
and comparison content first (closer to a lead), cost/informational content
next (highest search volume, longest to rank), hyperlocal content last
(smallest audience, highest conversion once found). No search-volume tool
was used to rank these; re-prioritize once real keyword data is available.

**Rule for every article below:** before publishing anything involving
costs, permit requirements, codes, or timelines, verify against current
authoritative sources (Clark County / City of Las Vegas building
department, NSCB) or your own completed-project data. Do not estimate or
invent figures.
