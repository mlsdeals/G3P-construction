# Backlink / Local Authority Strategy

Durable, legitimate link-building for a local construction brand — no
purchased links, PBNs, link farms, or mass directory spam.

## Near-term (first 3 months)

- **NSCB and local business directories**: ensure the Nevada State
  Contractors Board listing, local chamber of commerce (if joined), and any
  BBB profile all link to the website with consistent NAP.
- **Supplier/manufacturer directories**: if G3P uses specific material or
  fixture brands in its work, many manufacturers run "find a pro" /
  dealer-locator pages — legitimate, relevant links.
- **Realtor partnerships**: realtors G3P works with can link to the site
  from their own "preferred vendors" or "resources" pages — ask directly
  once a few completed projects exist to point to.

## Medium-term (3–9 months)

- **Original resources worth citing**: a real, data-backed cost guide or
  contractor-hiring checklist (see `CONTENT_PLAN_12MO.md`'s "original SEO
  assets" section) earns links naturally from local blogs, realtor sites,
  and homeowner forums — once it's genuinely useful, not before.
- **Local press / community involvement**: project features, any
  charitable or community work, local home-improvement publications.
- **Architecture/design partnerships**: if G3P collaborates with designers
  or architects on projects, cross-linking between project write-ups is
  natural and relevant.

## Ongoing

- **Google Business Profile** stays accurate and active (see
  `LOCAL_SEO_CHECKLIST.md`) — not a backlink, but it compounds with
  everything above for local visibility.
- **Internal content** (the 12-month plan) is itself a long-term authority
  play — topically deep, genuinely useful pages are what attract links
  without asking for them.

## Explicitly avoided

Buying links, private blog networks, link farms, mass/automated directory
submissions, and reciprocal-link schemes. None of these produce durable
authority, and several carry real penalty risk.
